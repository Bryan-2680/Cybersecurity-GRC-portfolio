### Access Control Assessment – Least Privilege & Segregation of Duties
A risk-based assessment of access-control weaknesses in a role-based enterprise environment, focused on excessive permissions, segregation of duties, and access
governance.

#### What it demonstrates

- Identifying access risks: excessive permissions, segregation of duties (SoD) conflicts, weak joiner/mover/leaver processes, and over-broad data sharing
- A worked example of an SoD conflict (a procurement-to-finance mover retaining create rights and gaining approval rights)
- Mapping controls to least privilege, RBAC, NIST SP 800-53 AC-6, and ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A

#### Key concepts: least privilege, segregation of duties, RBAC, joiner/mover/leaver,access reviews, qualitative likelihood × impact rating.

Demonstrative scenario based on common access-control risks in role-based ERP systems. Anonymised and illustrative.
